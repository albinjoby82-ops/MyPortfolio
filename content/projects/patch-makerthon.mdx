## What it was

A fast build night for students, engineers and creators who like shipping things. Teams of three worked through a 2½-hour sprint, either arriving together or forming a team on the night. It was beginner-friendly: bring your ideas and your energy.

The challenge was to hack Arduinos together into custom game controllers.

## What teams got

Every team had what it needed to get going: Arduino kits, a month of free access to Lovable, mentors from Patch's alumni, merch, spot prizes and plenty of pizza.

## Prizes

More than €250 of prizes were up for grabs, from mini-challenges to a first prize for the most creative approach to the theme, which was announced 24 hours before the event.

## Patch

Patch is a programme for people who want to build ambitious projects and connect with a community of makers. The event was run by Patch alumni, with UCD ElecSoc co-hosting and providing the space.

## What I did

I helped organise the Makerthon, and on the day I taught students how to build their controllers.

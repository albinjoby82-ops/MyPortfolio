## One mission

ElecSoc's mission is to inspire people to bring crazy ideas to life. MakerLabs is how that happens in practice: a workshop every couple of weeks where anyone can come and pick up the hard and soft skills engineering needs, from microcontrollers to motors, soldering to 3D printing, programming to digital design.

Every lab has a pre-written course page with the tasks, the code and a post-lab blog, so nobody is left guessing what to do next. The space and the funding are provided, so learning stays accessible to everyone.

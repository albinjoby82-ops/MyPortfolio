## One mission

ElecSoc's mission is to inspire people to bring crazy ideas to life. MakerLabs is how that happens in practice: workshops where anyone can come and pick up the hard and soft skills engineering needs, from microcontrollers to motors, soldering to 3D printing, programming to digital design.

Every lab has a pre-written course page with the tasks, the code and a post-lab blog, so nobody is left guessing what to do next. The space and the funding are provided, so learning stays accessible to everyone.

## What I did

Since joining, I have worked on the course material the labs run from. In January 2026 I updated the layout of the Lab 1 material, and in February I wrote the introduction for Lab 5, Good Waves, Bad Vibes (learning to read an oscilloscope), and set up its Zoom page.

## What it was

An expo to round off the year: activities, showcases, prizes and food, built for anyone who wants to see what people make in their spare time. Anyone could come, and entry was free. Visitors who just wanted to see the projects could take a spectator ticket.

## Demos

Anyone with something to show could apply to demo it, however big or small and whether it was a side project or a physical demo for a thesis. Each demo got a booth, coverage on ElecSoc's socials, the attention of people working in the industry, and goodies.

## Four robotics projects

Four ElecSoc robotics projects were presented on the day:

- **Robot Spider**, by Kelvin Gao: 24 servos and an FPV camera.
- **Desktop Robot Jarvis**, by Mike Chen: a screen on wheels that can control your home.
- **Analogue Kick Drum**, by Cian Brady: real musical beats made without any binary.
- **Sand Art Machine**, by Liam O'Sullivan: a machine that can draw any art in sand.

## Companies

MANNA Air Delivery came along with their delivery drone, giving visitors the chance to talk to the people building Ireland's new sector in the air. Other companies joined too.

## Prizes

Everyone who signed up automatically got a raffle ticket for a brand-new Bambu Lab 3D printer. Mini-competitions and challenges at the expo earned extra tickets.

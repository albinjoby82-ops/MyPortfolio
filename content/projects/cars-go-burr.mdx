## What I did

I did the hardware: I designed and 3D printed every car, and designed the circuits that go in them. [Joe Biju](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joebiju456/) wrote [the firmware](https://github.com/UCDElecSoc/WifiRCCars/tree/main) that runs on them.

## The brief

Build your very own remote-control car. Each team was given all the hardware it needed to build a small car around an ESP32, and the firmware was provided. The car was driven from a game controller: bring your own Xbox or PlayStation controller, connect it to a laptop, and control the car remotely.

It was open to everyone, with no experience needed. For the regulars there were extra challenges.
